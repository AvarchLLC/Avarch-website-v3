About our founder

Pooja Ranjan is the Herder-in-Chief of Ethereum Cat Herders and the Founder of Avarch LLC. She became part of the blockchain community in 2016 by learning about the Ethereum blockchain. In 2017, she founded Avarch LLC with EtherWorld as her first project to create awareness about blockchain technology. Publishing technical content, project updates, and the latest news, EtherWorld has grown to become one of the leading blockchain publishing websites.

In 2020, with the preparation for Ethereum 2.0, BlockAction was launched as an explorer to support the Beacon chain multi-client testnet since the launch of the Witti testnet. The explorer is currently upgraded to provide critical information on Beacon chain mainnet transactions.

Pooja actively contributes to the Ethereum Improvement Proposal (EIP) process and EIP data analytics, publishing a monthly EIPs statistics report. To extend continued support to the Ethereum community, she recently launched EIPsInsight, a comprehensive data analytics dashboard for EIPs.

As the Herder-in-Chief at Ethereum Cat Herders, a decentralized project management group supporting the Ethereum Protocol, she is the host of the highly popular education series PEEPanEIP and continues to support Ethereum network upgrades globally.
